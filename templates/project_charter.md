# Project Charter

## 1. Project Overview
- **Project Name**: Apex Integration - II
- **Sponsor**: Jonathan Reed, VP Enterprise Systems
- **Project Manager**: Henry
- **Date**: Nov 17, 2025

### 1.1 Background
Apex aggregates data from enterprise applications and provides unified insights across business lines, regions, and revenue segments. The Apex Integration – II initiative aims to enhance data ingestion from Sales, Finance, and Business Development systems, enabling faster analytics, improved forecasting, and improved decision-making dashboards.

### 1.2 Objectives & Success Criteria
- Objective 1: Integrate additional enterprise data sources (ERP, CRM, Pipeline Tools) into the Apex platform with automated refresh cycles.
- Objective 2: Improve dashboard performance and accuracy of business insights across global regions.
- Success will be measured by:
  - 20% improvement in dashboard response times  
  - 100% integration of identified data sources  
  - Reduction in manual reporting by 60%  
  - Executive satisfaction score > 4.5/5 after rollout  

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In-Scope
- Integration of Sales, Finance, and Business Development datasets  
- Enhancements to ETL/ELT workflows and metadata logging  
- Updates to business-line dashboards and KPI definitions  
- UAT cycles with regional business teams  
- Deployment of refreshed Apex UI modules  

### 2.2 Out-of-Scope
- New downstream product development  
- Any infrastructure upgrades outside the Apex environment  
- Integration enhancements for legacy systems scheduled for retirement
